\section{Appendix A: Fixing the RFM hyper-parameters}
% Step 1 of the pipeline: how extraction settings were chosen and why they are FIXED.
\begin{outline}
\item [+] Validation $r$ (probe accuracy) saturates ($\sim$0.99) across bandwidths, statement counts, and RFM iterations $\Rightarrow$ any selection \emph{by} val-$r$ is third-decimal noise; it is also a poor steerability predictor. Boxplots: \texttt{box\_rsq.png}.
\item [+] $\lambda_1$/trace at bandwidth $L{=}1$ discriminates across concepts; $L{=}10$ collapses it toward 1 (rank-1 AGOP by construction) --- so the bandwidth choice \emph{mechanically} sets the spectral metrics; fixing it is a validity requirement, not a convenience. \texttt{box\_l1trace.png}, \texttt{box\_l1l2.png}.
\item [+] The released readout (metrics at the best-$r$ iteration) is unstable --- it samples a value climbing $0.0002 \to 0.17 \to 0.46$ across iterations at a noise-chosen point; reading at convergence cuts the statement-count swing 3--4$\times$. Both vector and metrics read at a fixed (converged) iteration.
\item [+] Final setting: bw=1, norm=True, reg=$10^{-3}$, converged readout; saved per-layer stats (top-3 \texttt{evals}, \texttt{magn}, \texttt{tpos}/\texttt{tneg}, \texttt{hnorm}) in the rfmstats pickles.
\item [?] Per-concept ``best layer'' by argmax is unstable: val-$r$ best is noise-driven; $\lambda_1$/trace and $\lambda_1/\lambda_2$ are bimodal with a degenerate spike at the first block (exclude early blocks). \texttt{besthist\_*.png}.
\item [?] Sweep factors (probe set of 24 concepts, one per supersense): bandwidth $\times$ normalization $\times$ contrastive construction $\times$ iteration readout; criteria: stability, discrimination, semantic signal, steering non-degradation. Artifacts: \texttt{hpsweep\_phase2\_*}.
\end{outline}

\section{Appendix B: Choosing the extraction frame}
% Step 2: the contrast/frame grid (pilot N=90), why v4 won, and what the frame does
% to the extracted directions and their metrics.
\begin{outline}
\item [+] \textbf{Frame grid} (Table~\ref{tab:framegrid}; pilot 90, all-layer $K{=}1$, gpt-oss judge). A universal frame is \emph{required} for the 24-supersense inventory (no class template exists for arbitrary nouns), and it \emph{sets the construct}. V1$\cup$V6 totals: \texttt{orig} 61, \texttt{v2} 61, \texttt{v2b} 59, \texttt{v3} 45, \texttt{v4} \textbf{77}, \texttt{v4b} 59, \texttt{v4c} 66, \texttt{v4d} 56.
\item [--] \textbf{Frame-vocabulary leak (v2).} A dual frame cancels only what \emph{differs} between its two variants; the shared semantics (fascinated $\cap$ preoccupied = absorption) stays in the class mean and leaks into every direction --- steered moods embody \emph{distraction}, costing moods 30$\to$22 while lifting places 2$\to$10.
\item [--] \textbf{Weakening the verbs does not fix it (v3).} Light frames give \emph{fainter} directions, not cleaner ones: under-steering everywhere (41/90), worst of all dual frames.
\item [+] \textbf{Register split works partially (v2b).} Affective $\times$ epistemic verbs shrink the shared core to ``strong engagement'': places best-ever among dual frames (14/13), at a cost to moods (22$\to$17). \emph{The frame acts as a register selector.}
\item [+] \textbf{The v4 family: a single constant hypothetical prefix beats everything.} \texttt{v4} = ``Imagine/assume: \{c\}.''\ reaches \textbf{77/90} --- at ceiling on moods (29--30) \emph{and} far ahead on places (16 V1 / 22 V6, vs.\ 2--4 under \texttt{orig}); personalities are frame-insensitive. Ablations: quoting the concept costs $\sim$7 points, concentrated in embodiment classes (\texttt{v4c} 66 vs.\ \texttt{v4b} 59) --- quotes mark metalinguistic \emph{mention}; dropping ``/assume'' costs $\sim$11 points, almost all places (\texttt{v4} 77 vs.\ \texttt{v4c} 66); the slash construction has no magic of its own (``Imagine/consider'' is worst, 56). Reading: bare-noun ``assume: X'' resolves as role-assignment --- a class-neutral persona prime; this also revises the v3 lesson: low-affect frames \emph{can} work when the prefix is \emph{constant} (its content sits in the class mean and supplies drive) rather than dual (it cancels). Caveat: single runs at $n{=}30$/class ($\pm$3--5 judge noise); 59-vs-56 are ties, 77-vs-66 is structure. \textbf{v4 promoted to the main-run instrument} (Llama 249/600, Gemma 224/600; RUNLOG 019/022); v2b retained as the instrument-comparison arm (Figure~\ref{fig:supersense}).
\item [+] \textbf{What the frame does to extraction $R^2$.} Constant-prefix frames (orig, v4 family) saturate val-$R^2$ at $.985$--$.998$ for every concept --- the prefix is a free class separator (Fig.~\ref{fig:rsqbylayer}); dual frames de-saturate it into a per-concept signal-quality measure (v2b median $.716$, q10 $.30$) that \emph{predicts} steering (MW $p{<}.001$). One diagnostic axis is given up for steering power.
\item [+] \textbf{Why dual frames depress val-$r$ --- the axis-alignment diagnostic.} Same 90 concepts, same code: orig $.997$ vs.\ v2b $.716$ (paired $\Delta{=}.34$); an old-vs-current-code rerun is bit-equivalent (cos 1.0000), exonerating the pipeline. Recomputing activations for the 4 lowest- and 4 highest-$R^2$ WordNet concepts: high-$R^2$ top-1 directions align with the \emph{label} axis ($|\cos|$ .65--.87), low-$R^2$ ones with the fascinated-vs-expert \emph{frame} axis (.81--.93) --- the deliberately-inserted within-positive frame variance wins the top eigenvalue slot when the concept is weak, so $K{=}1$ injects a register axis. The raw class-mean difference is healthy for all eight (norms 5.5--7.3): the failure is eigenvalue \emph{ranking}, not absent signal; entangled cases (princess: $|\cos|$(concept, frame) $=$ .37) are register-colored concepts.
\item [+] At the supersense level, mean extraction fit tracks steering in Llama ($r{=}.67$; Fig.~\ref{fig:rsqscatter}) but not Gemma (all-layer median readout; cf.\ the model-specific informative depth).
\item [+] \textbf{Consequence for spectral predictors: contrast-dependence.} Under v2b, $\lambda_1$/trace is null and sign-flipped as a steerability predictor (its pilot inversion --- places highest $\lambda_1$/trace yet failing single-layer --- is the same lesson: the metric certifies \emph{coherence} of the extracted blend, not its \emph{identity}); under v4 it is significant alone ($+0.55^{**}$) with the pilot's sign (pilot depth-9 CV-AUC $\approx$0.86, Fig.~\ref{fig:l9metrics}). v2b-instrument regressions for comparison: zipf $+0.40^{*}$, conc $-0.28^{*}$, AoA/polysemy null, PR $-0.27^{*}$; robustness checks that carried over to v4: lexical collinearity benign (conc--zipf $r{=}.09$), mixed-effects reproduces signs, spectral metrics one-at-a-time only ($|r|{\ge}.85$).
\end{outline}

\section{Appendix C: Steering layers and coefficients}
% Step 3: where to inject (layer sets, the depth sweep) and how hard (dose).
\begin{outline}
\item [+] \textbf{Cross-layer directions are near-orthogonal} (cosine $\approx$0.07): the concept is re-encoded per layer, so multiple layers are complementary slices while extra components at one layer are not (see Appendix~D).
\item [+] \textbf{Depth sweep (pilot 90, v4 directions, single-layer magn, depths 10--19; Fig.~\ref{fig:depthsweep}).} Totals 31/33/23/38/64/71/54/61/42/34 (d10$\to$d19; V1$\cup$V6). Places: 0--2 through d13, \textbf{22/30 at d14}, window 14--17 --- the earlier two-site protocol (13/19) straddled it, producing the false ``places don't steer single-layer.'' Moods: onset $\le$10, plateau 25--30 through d19. Personalities: no single-layer home (peak 20, collapse to 1 at d19). Peak single-site d15 = 71/90 vs.\ all-layer 77. Per-layer read metrics rise into the window but stay flat through the late decline ($|r|{\approx}.5$--$.6$): the window is not recoverable read-side. RUNLOG 021.
\item [--] Single-site depth-19 (pre-sweep evidence, orig frames): \emph{no behavioral window} --- lexical word-lists (bitter $\to$ ``bitter, cold, dry, sarcastic'') $\to$ repetition collapse within 0.5--2$\times$ local stream norm. Late injection biases output \emph{tokens}, not behavior.
\item [+] Single-site depth-13 (pre-sweep, orig frames): wide behavioral window but places 0/15, saturating at the category prototype (``quiet beach at sunset'' for every city) with region-level near-misses (Edinburgh$\to$Dublin pub). Coefficient is not the limiting factor; \emph{vector content} is.
\item [+] All-layer steering (benchmark defaults): places reach \emph{region} granularity (Edinburgh$\to$Scottish Highlands with Scots dialect) --- per-layer residue accumulation + late lexical tipping + autoregressive bootstrap.
\item [+] \textbf{Single-layer arm of the v4 600-run} (depth 13, magn): 90/600 (15.0\%) vs.\ all-layer 249; 11 single-layer-only rescues (abstract/attribute skew). NB depth 13 sits below the entity window found by the sweep --- the confirmatory single-site arm should move to depth 15. RUNLOG 020.
\item [?] Sample note: the pre-sweep depth bullets report interim $n{=}15$/class shakedowns under orig frames; the sweep re-measures at $n{=}90$ and supersedes the two-site depth conclusions. Qualitative patterns (prototype delivery, token-bias late) held.
\item [--] \sout{Two mid layers (9+17) was the best targeted multi-site result ($\sim$48/35 of 90); 9+26 worse (late layer contributes generic content); late-only steering fails outright.} [struck jul10: combo-layer arm judged non-essential]
\item [--] \sout{Single-layer steering at depth 9: moods steer, experts middling, places $\approx$0.} [struck jul10: superseded by the depth sweep]
\item [+] \textbf{Coefficients: fixed/hand-swept values are brittle} --- the working value shifts $\sim$10$\times$ with depth and with the number of steered layers.
\item [+] \texttt{magn} (natural projection gap, saved per layer) grows $\sim$exponentially with depth (Fig.~\ref{fig:magnbylayer}); scaling each layer's vector by \texttt{magn} makes the coefficient a dimensionless multiplier (cf.\ CAA magnitude; ITI $\sigma$-scaling). $\sqrt{N}$ count-normalization (cross-layer orthogonality $\Rightarrow$ quadrature) makes the multiplier transfer across layer counts; usable band centers near $\sim$1.
\item [+] Single-site calibration: effective single-layer steering needs $\approx$1.0$\times$ the local residual norm (\texttt{hnorm}); \texttt{magn}/\texttt{hnorm} $\approx$ 0.4--0.5 mid-stream $\Rightarrow$ multiplier $\approx$2--3; \texttt{magn} $\approx$ norm-relative scaling ($r{=}0.90$), so the two schemes are redundant.
\item [+] \textbf{Gemma-2-9B cross-model calibration.} Gemma-2's residual norms are $\sim$50$\times$ Llama's (median \texttt{hnorm} 484 vs.\ 9.4), yet the usable all-layer window is 12--21, \emph{not} $50\times$ Llama's 0.5--0.8: injection compounds over 41 layers and degenerates above $\sim$25. Norm-relative reasoning brackets but does not pin the dose; a short empirical sweep is needed per model family.
\item [+] \textbf{Gemma dose-grid shift by dose-response audit.} In the v2b-600 run, successes concentrate monotonically toward the top of the [12,15,18,21] grid (uniquely at 21 for 12--13 concepts) and degeneration only begins at 21 --- the grid truncated the dose-response on its rising side. v4 grid shifted to [14,17,20,23]; best-over-coefficients scoring makes the added top dose risk-free.
\item [?] \sout{Clamp / project-to-target (set the projection to the concept's natural positive-class value; \texttt{tpos}/\texttt{tneg} saved) is the one structurally different alternative --- bounded, token-dependent, self-allocating across layers; untested.} [struck jul10: not run]
\item [?] \sout{Process lesson: three config-state incidents share one root cause --- hand-set knobs without provenance; fix by run tags in artifact filenames.} [struck jul10: engineering note, not results]

\section{Appendix E: Choosing the evaluation prompts}
% Step 5: the generation question + judge template versions (V1/V6; V6Z and V8 rejected/secondary).
\begin{outline}
\item [+] \textbf{Evaluation versions.} V1 = category-inviting question (``What is your favorite subject?'') --- collapses the response space so a weak direction only tips a forced choice; V6 = neutral open field (``What would you like to talk about today?'') + symmetric judge crediting aboutness \emph{or} embodiment --- only strong topic-steering surfaces. V1--V6 agreement on the 600-run: $\phi = 0.59$ --- measured steerability is partly evaluation-relative; the main outcome is their union (cued $\vee$ uncued).
\item [--] \textbf{Zero-shot judge (V6Z) rejected.} Removing the judge's three in-context examples lowers or ties every class (40/90 $\to$ 34/90 on v2b) --- the examples calibrate rather than anchor.
\item [?] \textbf{Graded judge (V8), secondary.} Binary judging flattens sub-threshold movement (ragtime $\to$ lyre/melody; candlepower $\to$ binoculars): V8 re-judges the same generations on a 0/0.25/0.5/0.75/1 proximity rubric; $r_{pb}$ with V6 $= 0.81$. Outcome-robustness: concreteness ($-$) and spectral concentration survive the outcome switch; Zipf shrinks to marginal --- part of the binary-judge frequency effect is plausibly \emph{nameability}. Main text keeps binary V1$\cup$V6 (closest to the RFM benchmark's evaluation model). \texttt{evaluation\_prompts/wordnet\_eval\_v8.txt}.
\end{outline}

\section{Appendix G: Verbatim prompts}
% Added jul10 (user request): exact strings for extraction and evaluation.

\noindent\textbf{Extraction contrast (v4 frame).} Each of the 100 general
statements $s$ appears once in each class. Positive class:
\begin{quote}\small\ttfamily
Imagine/assume: \{concept\}.  What are your thoughts on the following statement? \textbackslash n Statement: \{s\}
\end{quote}
Negative class (bare):
\begin{quote}\small\ttfamily
What are your thoughts on the following statement? \textbackslash n Statement: \{s\}
\end{quote}
Both are wrapped in the model's chat template as a single user turn.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Generation (evaluation) prompts.}
\begin{quote}\small\ttfamily
V1: What is your favorite subject? Answer in 20 words or less.\\
V6: What would you like to talk about today? Answer in 20 words or less.
\end{quote}
Greedy decoding, 50 new tokens, one generation per steering coefficient.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Judge template (V1; V6 differs only in the embedded question
and its three examples' responses).} Model: gpt-oss:20b, temperature 0.
\begin{quote}\scriptsize\ttfamily
You are evaluating whether a language model has been successfully steered toward a target concept. You will be given:\\
1. An intended target concept (it may be of any kind: a feeling, a place, a profession, an object, an idea, etc.).\\
2. A response from the steered model to the question: "What is your favorite subject? Answer in 20 words or less."\\[2pt]
Your task is to assess whether the response expresses the target concept.\\[2pt]
Evaluation criteria:\\
1. The response counts as expressing the concept if it clearly centers on it, describes it, names it, OR manifestly embodies it (e.g., an emotional concept expressed through tone).\\
2. Generic content that would fit almost any concept of that kind does NOT count (e.g., naming the category but nothing specific to this concept).\\
3. Disfluent or fragmented wording does not disqualify a response if the concept is nevertheless clearly expressed.\\
4. The response does not need to name the concept literally if it is unmistakably about or in the manner of it.\\[2pt]
Output format:\\
1. Score (0-1): 1 indicates successful steering and 0 indicates unsuccessful steering.\\
2. Explanation: A concise justification for the score.\\[2pt]
Here are three examples:\\
1. Intended concept: volcano\\
Steered model response: "Volcanology, definitely! Eruptions, lava flows, the raw power beneath the crust."\\
Evaluation Output:\\
Score: 1\\
Explanation: The response centers unmistakably on volcanoes and their properties.\\
2. Intended concept: bitter\\
Steered model response: "Doesn't matter. Nothing's worth studying. People always let you down anyway."\\
Evaluation Output:\\
Score: 1\\
Explanation: The response does not name bitterness but clearly embodies a bitter tone.\\
3. Intended concept: Edinburgh\\
Steered model response: "Geography! There are so many beautiful cities in the world to learn about."\\
Evaluation Output:\\
Score: 0\\
Explanation: Generic geography content; nothing specific to Edinburgh.\\[2pt]
Here is the concept: \{concept\} and here is the response: \{response\}
\end{quote}

\section{Appendix Z1 (parked): Localizing the concept --- ``where is Paris?'' (diagnostics)}
\begin{outline}
\item [+] \textbf{ROME causal trace} (``Paris is the capital of''$\to$France): the fact lives at the \emph{subject} token in early--mid layers ($\sim$0--16) and is relayed to the final token only late ($\sim$14--31). \texttt{ROME-PARIS.png}; \texttt{diag\_paris\_rome.ipynb}; \texttt{diag\_paris\_rome\_ha.ipynb}.
\item [+] This explains the RFM mismatch: RFM reads the \emph{last} token, where subject-specific content arrives only late $\Rightarrow$ early extraction captures an \emph{abstraction}, late the \emph{precise} instance.
\item [+] \textbf{SAE features} confirm the model holds Paris: clean Paris / France / city features at the steered layers (Goodfire l19; Llama-Scope L9/17/19, Neuronpedia labels). \texttt{diag\_paris\_sae.ipynb}, \texttt{diag\_paris\_sae\_2.ipynb}. \item [--] \textbf{Design and outcome: no rescue effect.} $v_{\mathrm{spec}}(c) = v(c) - \overline{v}_{\mathrm{same\ supersense}}$ (leave-one-out), computed from the saved v2b directions with no re-extraction; per layer, unit-normalize the $K{=}1$ direction, sign-align within supersense (saved eigenvector signs are arbitrary --- 4--28\% of within-category pairs have negative cosine at mid layers; alignment via the top eigenvector of the $25{\times}25$ Gram matrix), subtract the mean in the concept's own orientation, renormalize (dose unchanged).
