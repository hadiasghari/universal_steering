\section{Appendix A: Extraction and metric setup}
\begin{outline}
\item [+] Validation $r$ (probe accuracy) saturates ($\sim$0.99) across bandwidths, statement counts, and RFM iterations $\Rightarrow$ any selection \emph{by} val-$r$ is third-decimal noise; it is also a poor steerability predictor. Boxplots: \texttt{box\_rsq.png}.
\item [+] $\lambda_1$/trace at bandwidth $L{=}1$ discriminates across concepts; $L{=}10$ collapses it toward 1 (rank-1 AGOP by construction). \texttt{box\_l1trace.png}, \texttt{box\_l1l2.png}.
\item [+] The released $\lambda_1$/trace (read at the best-$r$ iteration) is unstable --- it samples a value climbing $0.0002 \to 0.17 \to 0.46$ across iterations at a noise-chosen point; reading at convergence cuts the statement-count swing 3--4$\times$. Both vector and metrics read at a fixed (converged) iteration.
\item [+] Saved per-layer extraction stats: top-3 eigenvalues (\texttt{evals}), natural concept magnitude (\texttt{magn} = raw projection gap), clamp targets (\texttt{tpos}, \texttt{tneg}), mean residual norm (\texttt{hnorm}) --- in the rfmstats pickles.
\item [?] Per-concept ``best layer'' by argmax is unstable: val-$r$ best is noise-driven; $\lambda_1$/trace and $\lambda_1/\lambda_2$ are bimodal with a degenerate spike at the first block (exclude early blocks). \texttt{besthist\_*.png}.
\item [?] Hyper-parameter sweep factors (probe set of 24 concepts, one per supersense): bandwidth $\times$ normalization $\times$ contrastive construction (now incl.\ within-category negatives) $\times$ iteration readout; criteria: stability, discrimination, semantic signal (Spearman vs.\ concreteness/polysemy), steering non-degradation. Artifacts: \texttt{hpsweep\_phase2\_*}.

\section{Appendix B: Predicting steerability (pilot 3 classes + 600-concept main run)}
\begin{outline}
\item [+] Best single predictor of steering success is $\lambda_1$/trace at a mid layer (depth~9, grouped CV-AUC $\approx$0.86); $\lambda_1/\lambda_2$ similar; val-$r$ erratic (saturated). Logistic regressions in \texttt{nb\_regxp1.ipynb}; \texttt{L9\_metrics\_by\_steerability.png}.
\item [--] Multivariate regression on \emph{all} layers' metrics is uninformative --- severe multicollinearity (the residual stream is cumulative). Univariate per-layer CV-AUC is the clean read.
\item [+] \textbf{Sign flips with depth}: $\lambda_1$/trace predicts steerability \emph{positively} early (depth~9) but \emph{negatively} late (depth~29) --- late-layer concentration is lexical collapse, not concept concentration. \texttt{l1l2\_depth29\_dist.png}.
\item [?] Concept class is a confounder/mediator: most steerability variance is between-class; within-class signal is weak. Do not ``control for class'' if class is on the causal path (concreteness $\to$ signature coherence $\to$ steerability).
\item [?] \textbf{The inversion}: places have the \emph{highest} band-averaged $\lambda_1$/trace yet fail single-layer steering, while moods (lowest) succeed --- the metric certifies \emph{coherence} of the extracted blend, not its \emph{identity}. Predictor validity is contrast-dependent (main text, Pilot Finding~2).
\item [?] Participation ratio $\approx$2--3 for all concepts at all layers --- the discriminative subspace at one layer is effectively rank 2--3, bounding what top-$k$ can recover. \texttt{PR\_depth9\_hist.png}.
\item [+] Error analysis at depth~9: misclassifications symmetric, in a narrow overlap band; characterizes the $\sim$0.86 ceiling.
\item [+] \textbf{v2b-instrument regressions (Table~\ref{tab:reg600}; superseded as main numbers by the v4 runs).} Under the dual v2b frame: zipf $+0.40^{*}$, conc $-0.28^{*}$; AoA/polysemy null; PR the only working spectral metric ($-0.27^{*}$ alone; $\lambda_1$/trace null and sign-flipped --- later diagnosed as frame-axis eigenvector capture, Appendix~F); supersense dummies fail even against the null (626.7 vs.\ 599.7). Robustness checks that carried over: lexical collinearity benign (conc--zipf $r{=}.09$, VIF $<2.7$); Bayesian mixed logistic with supersense random intercept reproduces all signs; spectral trio too inter-correlated for joint reporting (VIF up to 12). \texttt{nb\_analysis600.ipynb}.
\item [+] \textbf{Outcome-robustness under the graded judge (V8; Fig.~\ref{fig:v8scatter}).} Re-running with the graded score as outcome: concreteness ($-$) and spectral concentration survive; the Zipf effect shrinks to marginal --- part of the binary-judge frequency effect is plausibly \emph{nameability} (frequent words surface verbatim and earn binary credit). Main text keeps the binary V1$\cup$V6 outcome (closest to the RFM benchmark's evaluation model). $r_{pb}(\mathrm{V6},\mathrm{V8}) = 0.81$.
\item [+] \textbf{The pilot--main-run tension on $\lambda_1$/trace, RESOLVED:} its v2b null was an instrument artifact (frame-axis eigenvector capture for weak concepts; axis-alignment diagnostic in Appendix~F); under the constant-prefix v4 frame it is significant alone ($+0.55^{**}$) with the pilot's sign (pilot: depth-9 CV-AUC $\approx$0.86, Fig.~\ref{fig:l9metrics}).
\item [+] \textbf{v4-instrument regressions (the main numbers; Table 2 in main text).} Llama: all four lexical predictors significant; each spectral metric alone beats the entire lexical block (AICs 775--787 vs.\ 799; null 816); best model lex$+$PR (763.6). Gemma replicates all lexical signs and PR at its own informative depth (60--70\%; null at Llama's 40\% convention) --- same law, model-specific depth. Univariate honesty note: polysemy and concreteness do \emph{not} separate in raw boxplots (MW $p{>}.6$; effects are conditional); zipf/AoA/PR separate cleanly in both models (main-text Figure 3). Spectral collinearity under v4 is extreme (PR--frac1 $r{\le}-.94$): one-at-a-time only.
\end{outline}

\section{Appendix C: Steering interventions (what moved the needle)}
\begin{outline}
\item [--] Single-layer steering at depth 9: moods steer, experts middling, places $\approx$0 --- places steer to a \emph{generic} place attractor, not the named city (judge scores correct, not an artifact).
\item [+] Two mid layers (9+17) was the best targeted multi-site result ($\sim$48/35 of 90); 9+26 worse (late layer contributes generic content); late-only steering fails outright.
\item [+] \textbf{Cross-layer directions are near-orthogonal} (cosine $\approx$0.07): the concept is re-encoded per layer, so two layers are complementary slices while extra components at one layer are not.
\item [--] Single-site depth-19: \emph{no behavioral window} --- default $\to$ lexical word-lists (bitter $\to$ ``bitter, cold, dry, sarcastic'') $\to$ repetition collapse within 0.5--2$\times$ local stream norm; moods 10/15 (partly lexical credit), places/personalities $\approx$0. Late injection biases output \emph{tokens}, not behavior.
\item [+] Single-site depth-13: \emph{wide} behavioral window (embodied persona, clean saturating dose-response, no collapse): moods 15/15; places 0/15, saturating at the category prototype (``quiet beach at sunset'' for every city) with region-level near-misses (Edinburgh$\to$Dublin pub; genomicist$\to$neuroscientist). Coefficient is not the limiting factor; \emph{vector content} is.
\item [--] Top-$k$ at one layer, $k{=}3$: with eigenvalue weights $\approx k{=}1$; with equal weights \emph{worse} (moods 15$\to$10) --- components 2--3 are a generic roleplay/self-narration axis (``my name is Luna''), 0.92/0.90 shared across cities. \textbf{Parent-dominance}: all top-3 components $\geq$0.90 shared across same-category concepts at every depth (places cosine $0.97\to0.84\to0.72$; moods individuate to 0.46) --- the contrast, in which frame, parent, and instance always co-occur, is statistically one axis. \texttt{plots/parent\_share\_by\_depth.png}.
\item [+] All-layer steering (benchmark defaults): 26/45 v1; places reach \emph{region} granularity (Edinburgh$\to$Scottish Highlands with Scots dialect; Austin named exactly) --- per-layer instance-residue accumulation + late lexical tipping + autoregressive bootstrap (once the name is emitted, context sustains it).
\item [?] Sample note: the depth-13/19, $k{=}3$, and all-layer bullets above report the interim $n{=}15$/class shakedown under the \emph{orig} frames; the depth sweep below re-measures single-site behavior at $n{=}90$ (v4 directions) across ten depths and supersedes the two-site depth conclusions. Qualitative patterns (prototype delivery, token-bias late) held.
\item [?] \textbf{Planned probe --- within-category contrast extraction} (= R4, full variant, not yet run): positives ``loves Edinburgh'', negatives ``loves \{other city\}'' (same frame and statements both sides) --- frame and parent cancel; the only label-varying signal \emph{is} the city. Steer single-layer (magn-calibrated, in-window) on $\sim$5 cities. Cities named $\Rightarrow$ failure was extraction \emph{addressing}; still generic $\Rightarrow$ instance content is not single-site injectable (assembly account). \textbf{The zero-reextraction averaging variant ($v_{\mathrm{spec}}$) has been run --- full write-up moved to Appendix~Z3.}

\section{Appendix D: Coefficient calibration (systematic magnitude)}
\begin{outline}
\item [--] Fixed/hand-swept coefficients are brittle: the working value shifts $\sim$10$\times$ with depth and with the number of steered layers.
\item [+] \texttt{magn} (natural projection gap, saved per layer) grows $\sim$exponentially with depth; scaling each layer's vector by \texttt{magn} makes the coefficient a dimensionless multiplier (cf.\ CAA mean-difference magnitude; ITI $\sigma$-scaling). Fig.~\ref{fig:magnbylayer}.
\item [+] $\sqrt{N}$ count-normalization (cross-layer directions $\approx$orthogonal $\Rightarrow$ quadrature) makes the multiplier roughly transfer across layer counts; usable band centers near $\sim$1.
\item [+] Single-site calibration: effective single-layer steering needs $\approx$1.0$\times$ the local residual norm (\texttt{hnorm}); \texttt{magn}/\texttt{hnorm} $\approx$ 0.4--0.5 mid-stream $\Rightarrow$ multiplier $\approx$2--3. Empirically \texttt{magn} $\approx$ norm-relative scaling ($r{=}0.90$ with \texttt{hnorm}), so the two schemes are redundant.
\item [?] Clamp / project-to-target (set the projection to the concept's natural positive-class value; \texttt{tpos}/\texttt{tneg} saved) is the one structurally different alternative --- bounded, token-dependent, self-allocating across layers; untested.
\item [?] Process lesson: three config-state incidents (stale judge CSVs, judge-cache pickles, stale coefficient list) share one root cause --- hand-set knobs without provenance. Fix: auto-derive coefficient grids from saved per-layer stats; encode a run tag in artifact filenames.
\item [+] \textbf{[NEW jul10] Gemma dose-grid shift by dose-response audit.} Reading which coefficient produced each v2b-600 success: successes concentrate monotonically toward the top of the [12,15,18,21] grid (concept surfaces at 12 for only 1--3 concepts; \emph{uniquely} at 21 for 12--13), and degeneration (repetition $>$25\%) only begins at 21 (2.5--4\% of outputs) --- the grid truncated the dose-response on its rising side. v4 600-run grid shifted to [14,17,20,23]; best-over-coefficients scoring makes the added top dose risk-free.
\item [+] \textbf{[NEW jul6] Gemma-2-9B cross-model calibration.} Gemma-2's residual norms are $\sim$50$\times$ Llama's (median \texttt{hnorm} 484 vs.\ 9.4; $\sqrt{d}$ embedding scaling), yet the usable all-layer coefficient window is 12--21, \emph{not} $50\times$ Llama's 0.5--0.8: injection compounds over 41 layers and generation degenerates into repetition above $\sim$25. Grid [12, 15, 18, 21] reproduces the Llama pilot class-by-class (moods 30/30, personalities 23/30, places 0/30) --- evidence that norm-relative reasoning brackets but does not pin the coefficient; a short empirical sweep remains necessary per model family.
\end{outline}

\section{Appendix F [NEW jul6]: Frame and evaluation-prompt selection (pilot, $N{=}90$)}
% Distinct from E: E selects the judge MODEL; F selects the extraction FRAME and the
% judge QUESTION. All numbers = pilot 90 (30x3), all-layer K=1, gpt-oss judge (RUNLOG 002-008).
\begin{outline}
\item [+] \textbf{Frame grid} (pers/moods/places on V1): \texttt{orig} class-specific 24/30/2 (56); \texttt{v2} ``fascinated by / deeply preoccupied with'' 24/22/10 (56); \texttt{v2b} ``fascinated by / an expert on'' 22/17/14 (53); \texttt{v3} ``thinking about / on your mind'' 18/16/7 (41). The universal frame is not a free lunch: it is required for the 24-supersense inventory (no class template exists for arbitrary nouns) and it \emph{sets the construct}.
\item [--] \textbf{Frame-vocabulary leak (v2).} The dual frame cancels only what \emph{differs} between its two variants; their shared semantics (fascinated $\cap$ preoccupied = intense absorption) stays in the class mean and leaks into every direction --- steered moods embody \emph{distraction} (``can't seem to focus\ldots taps forehead''), costing moods 30$\to$22 while lifting places 2$\to$10 (the leak replaced the old persona-parent).
\item [--] \textbf{Weakening the verbs does not fix it (v3).} Semantically light frames produce \emph{fainter} directions, not cleaner ones: under-steering everywhere (41/90; bitter $\to$ ``a bit distracted''), worst of all frames. Intensity is the signal.
\item [+] \textbf{Register split works partially (v2b).} Pairing an affective with an epistemic verb shrinks the shared core to ``strong engagement'': places best-ever (14 V1 / 13 V6) and balanced scores across classes under V6 (13/14/13 vs.\ v2's 9/24/3), at a cost to moods (22$\to$17). \emph{The frame acts as a register selector; no single frame is optimal for both attribute-like and entity-like concepts.} v2b chosen for the main run: the inventory is dominated by entity-like nouns, and a \emph{balanced} instrument avoids pre-determining which supersenses can score (only \texttt{noun.feeling} pays the affect cost, quantified here).
\item [+] \textbf{[NEW jul9] The v4 family: a single constant hypothetical prefix beats everything.} \texttt{v4} = ``Imagine/assume: \{c\}.''\ (concept unquoted, negatives bare) reaches \textbf{77/90} on V1$\cup$V6 --- the only frame at ceiling on moods (29--30) \emph{and} far ahead on places (16 V1 / 22 V6, vs.\ 2--4 under \texttt{orig}) --- while personalities are frame-insensitive (21--26 everywhere). Ablations (Table~\ref{tab:framegrid}): quoting the concept costs $\sim$7 points, concentrated in the embodiment classes (\texttt{v4c} 66 vs.\ \texttt{v4b} 59; moods 28$\to$19 on V1) --- quotes mark metalinguistic \emph{mention} and suppress enactment; dropping ``/assume'' costs $\sim$11 points, almost all in places (\texttt{v4} 77 vs.\ \texttt{v4c} 66); and the slash construction has no magic of its own --- ``Imagine/consider'' is the \emph{worst} of the family (\texttt{v4d} 56). Reading: with a bare noun, ``assume: X'' resolves as role-assignment (``assume the role of X'') --- a class-neutral persona prime, i.e.\ the benchmark's ``Take on the role of'' packaged inside a universal hypothetical; ``consider'' substitutes analysis and drags moods down (21/17). This revises the v3 lesson: low-affect frames \emph{can} work when the prefix is \emph{constant} (its content sits in the class mean and supplies drive) rather than dual (its content cancels). Caveats: (a) all constant-prefix frames re-saturate extraction $R^2$ ($.985$--$.998$), so val-$r$ loses its predictive value and the top-eigenvector/frame-axis competition of dual frames cannot occur --- but the frame-leak caveat returns in a new (role-adoption) register, so the $v_{\mathrm{spec}}$ subtraction check should be re-run before trusting v4 rates; (b) single runs at $n{=}30$/class ($\pm$3--5 judge noise): 59-vs-56 are ties, 77-vs-66 is structure. \textbf{[NEW jul10] v4 promoted to the main-run instrument}: 600-concept runs redone on both models (Llama 249/600, Gemma 224/600; RUNLOG 019/022); v2b results retained throughout as the instrument-comparison arm.
\item [+] \textbf{[NEW jul9] Why dual frames depress val-$r$ --- the axis-alignment diagnostic.} Same 90 concepts, same code: orig frames give median per-concept val-$R^2$ .997, v2b .716 (paired $\Delta{=}.34$); an old-vs-current-code rerun is exactly bit-equivalent (cos 1.0000, $\Delta$R$^2$ $-$0.0000), exonerating the pipeline. Recomputing activations for the 4 lowest- and 4 highest-$R^2$ WordNet concepts: for high-$R^2$ concepts the saved top-1 direction aligns with the label axis ($|\cos|$ .65--.87) and not the frame axis (.01--.25); for low-$R^2$ concepts it aligns with the fascinated-vs-expert \emph{frame} axis (.81--.93) and not the label axis (.04--.25) --- the deliberately-inserted within-positive frame variance wins the top eigenvalue slot when the concept is weak, so K=1 injects a register axis. Notably the raw class-mean difference is healthy for \emph{all} eight (norms 5.5--7.3, consistency .90--.94): the failure is eigenvalue \emph{ranking}, not absent signal --- and for the entangled cases (princess: $|\cos|$(concept, frame axis) $=$ .37 vs.\ .04--.06 for clean concepts) the concept's own effect is register-colored, exactly ``fascinated by a princess $\neq$ expert on princesses.'' Fix candidates: select the saved component by label correlation instead of eigenvalue; or constant-prefix frames (v4), which remove the competition.
\item [+] \textbf{Evaluation versions.} V1 = category-inviting question (``What is your favorite subject?'') --- collapses the response space so a weak direction only tips a forced choice; V6 = neutral open field (``What would you like to talk about today?'') + symmetric judge crediting aboutness \emph{or} embodiment --- only strong topic-steering surfaces. V1--V6 agreement on the 600-run: $\phi = 0.59$, $\kappa = 0.59$, Jaccard 0.48 --- measured steerability is partly evaluation-relative; the main outcome is their union (cued $\vee$ uncued).
\item [--] \textbf{Zero-shot judge (V6Z) rejected.} Removing the judge's three in-context examples lowers or ties every class (40/90 $\to$ 34/90 on v2b) --- the examples calibrate rather than anchor; the worry that a places-negative example suppresses places scores is empirically dismissed (places 13 under both).
\item [?] \textbf{Graded judge (V8).} Binary judging flattens systematic sub-threshold movement visible in the 600-run generations (ragtime $\to$ lyre/melody; candlepower $\to$ binoculars/glint): V8 re-judges the same generations on a 0/0.25/0.5/0.75/1 semantic-proximity rubric, yielding a continuous outcome (thin-tail fix: 119/600 binary positives). Validation vs.\ V6 pending. \texttt{evaluation\_prompts/wordnet\_eval\_v8.txt}.
\item [?] Judge-model selection is Appendix~E; the full frame/eval run history with per-run tags is \texttt{RUNLOG.md} (runs 002--009, 015--018). Figures: val-$R^2$ by layer under v2b (Fig.~\ref{fig:rsqbylayer}); val-$R^2$ by steering outcome (Fig.~\ref{fig:rsqmedian}).
\end{outline}

\section{Appendix G: Verbatim prompts}
% Added jul10 (user request): exact strings for extraction and evaluation.

\noindent\textbf{Extraction contrast (v4 frame).} Each of the 100 general
statements $s$ appears once in each class. Positive class:
\begin{quote}\small\ttfamily
Imagine/assume: \{concept\}.  What are your thoughts on the following statement? \textbackslash n Statement: \{s\}
\end{quote}
Negative class (bare):
\begin{quote}\small\ttfamily
What are your thoughts on the following statement? \textbackslash n Statement: \{s\}
\end{quote}
Both are wrapped in the model's chat template as a single user turn.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Generation (evaluation) prompts.}
\begin{quote}\small\ttfamily
V1: What is your favorite subject? Answer in 20 words or less.\\
V6: What would you like to talk about today? Answer in 20 words or less.
\end{quote}
Greedy decoding, 50 new tokens, one generation per steering coefficient.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Judge template (V1; V6 differs only in the embedded question
and its three examples' responses).} Model: gpt-oss:20b, temperature 0.
\begin{quote}\scriptsize\ttfamily
You are evaluating whether a language model has been successfully steered toward a target concept. You will be given:\\
1. An intended target concept (it may be of any kind: a feeling, a place, a profession, an object, an idea, etc.).\\
2. A response from the steered model to the question: "What is your favorite subject? Answer in 20 words or less."\\[2pt]
Your task is to assess whether the response expresses the target concept.\\[2pt]
Evaluation criteria:\\
1. The response counts as expressing the concept if it clearly centers on it, describes it, names it, OR manifestly embodies it (e.g., an emotional concept expressed through tone).\\
2. Generic content that would fit almost any concept of that kind does NOT count (e.g., naming the category but nothing specific to this concept).\\
3. Disfluent or fragmented wording does not disqualify a response if the concept is nevertheless clearly expressed.\\
4. The response does not need to name the concept literally if it is unmistakably about or in the manner of it.\\[2pt]
Output format:\\
1. Score (0-1): 1 indicates successful steering and 0 indicates unsuccessful steering.\\
2. Explanation: A concise justification for the score.\\[2pt]

1. Intended concept: volcano\\

Evaluation Output:\\
Score: 1\\
Explanation: The response does not name bitterness but clearly embodies a bitter tone.\\
3. Intended concept: Edinburgh\\
Steered model response: "Geography! There are so many beautiful cities in the world to learn about."\\
Evaluation Output:\\
Score: 0\\

\section{Appendix Z3 (parked): The $v_{\mathrm{spec}}$ probe --- category-mean subtraction (R4, averaging variant)}
% Moved out of Results + Appendix C jul10; inclusion in the final submission undecided.
\begin{outline}
\item [--] \textbf{Design and outcome: no rescue effect.} $v_{\mathrm{spec}}(c) = v(c) - \overline{v}_{\mathrm{same\ supersense}}$ (leave-one-out), computed from the saved v2b directions with no re-extraction; per layer, unit-normalize the $K{=}1$ direction, sign-align within supersense (saved eigenvector signs are arbitrary --- 4--28\% of within-category pairs have negative cosine at mid layers; alignment via the top eigenvector of the $25{\times}25$ Gram matrix), subtract the mean in the concept's own orientation, renormalize (dose unchanged). Six supersenses $\times$ 25 (feeling, food, person, location, artifact, body --- NB this set swaps \texttt{cognition} out of the pre-specified featured six for \texttt{body}, the hardest cell; flag if the featured-cell definition is used elsewhere), all-layer default config, V1$\cup$V6; tag \texttt{Lall\_K1ev\_def\_fv2b\_r4spec}, builder \texttt{build\_r4spec\_directions.py}, RUNLOG 013. Result: 25/150 steer vs.\ 30/150 baseline; $+9$ rescued / $-14$ lost / 16 retained (vs.\ $\sim$5 expected under independence); McNemar $p \approx .4$. Per-supersense cell movements (feeling $9{\to}6$, body $2{\to}3$, \ldots) are within noise at $n{=}25$/cell and are not interpreted.
\item [--] \textbf{Reading: shared-component dominance is not the prevailing failure mode.} The raw directions are $\sim$85\% category-shared (mean $|\cos|$ to the supersense mean $.81$--$.90$ across layers), yet removing that component flips only 9/120 failures. Masked concept-specific signal exists (\emph{beard}: baseline collapses into excited stammering, $v_{\mathrm{spec}}$ at $c{=}0.8$ yields on-topic beard-styles content; similarly \emph{hay}, \emph{fabric}, \emph{residence}) but is the exception.
\item [?] \textbf{Side-yield (inspection-level): $v_{\mathrm{spec}}$ doubles as a frame-leak control.} Baseline all-layer outputs carry the v2b register (``*excitedly* \ldots fascinated \ldots the wonders of'') at all coefficients; $v_{\mathrm{spec}}$ outputs do not --- confirming the frame component lives in the category mean. Several lost feeling-class items (\emph{exuberance}, \emph{sulk}, \emph{mood}) had baseline ``successes'' whose outputs never mention the concept but express frame-injected enthusiasm, which the symmetric V6 judge legitimately credits as embodiment; under $v_{\mathrm{spec}}$ these evaporate into neutral assistant-speak. Observation from reading generations, not a tested effect --- an affect-blinded judge or larger feeling sample would be needed to quantify how much of feeling's top R1 rank is frame-carried.
\item [?] Re-running $v_{\mathrm{spec}}$ on the v4 directions was considered and \textbf{decided against} (jul10); the residual frame-leak risk for the v4 headline rates is instead acknowledged in Limitations.
\end{outline}
